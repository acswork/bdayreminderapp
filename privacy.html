Birthdays Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

In short: Everything you add stays on your iPhone. We don't collect, store, sell, or share your personal information, and the app has no accounts, ads, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The names, dates (birthdays, anniversaries and other yearly dates), optional years, groups, and reminder settings you add are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to any server we operate. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Like other app data, this information may be included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) if you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and your device, not by us.

Contacts

If you choose Import, then From Contacts, the app asks for permission and then shows you the names, birthdays, and anniversaries saved in your contacts. Nothing is added until you pick who to add. The app only reads names and these dates, never changes your contacts, and never sends contact information off your iPhone. You can turn off access anytime in Settings.

Messages

The app can't read your messages. If you choose to set up an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app, Shortcuts passes the text of new messages that match words you pick (like "birthday") to the app. The app reads that text on your iPhone, keeps any dates it finds in a list for you to review, and discards the message text. Nothing is added until you choose. You can turn the automation off anytime in the Shortcuts app.

On-device AI

When you type or paste dates into the app, it may use Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence) to read them. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Your text is not sent to us or to any third party.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. We don't use push notification servers, and we never receive your reminder content. You choose whether to allow notifications, including Time Sensitive notifications, and can change this anytime in Settings.

Sharing and backups you create

You can choose to share a group, or save a backup file of your list. A shared group contains its members' names and dates (month and day only, no years). A backup file contains your full list and reminder settings. In both cases, you decide where it goes, for example a message, email, AirDrop, or the Files app. We never receive a copy.

If a shared group is sent as a link to our web page, the names and dates are stored in the part of the link after the "#" symbol, which web browsers do not send to the web server, so the page never sees them.

Crash reports and beta feedback

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports to help fix problems. If you're testing a beta through TestFlight, any feedback or screenshots you choose to send come to us through Apple. These are optional and controlled by you.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
